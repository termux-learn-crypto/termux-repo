Pyasa Kauwa

Ek baar garmi ke din the. Ek kauwa bahut pyasa tha. Woh paani dhoondhte dhoondhte bahut door udta raha. Aakhir use ek bagiche me ek matka dikha. Matke me paani tha, par bahut neeche tha. Kauwe ki chonch paani tak nahi pahunch rahi thi. Kauwe ne socha, ab kya karun? Phir use ek tarkeeb soojhi. Usne paas pade chhote chhote kankar uthaye aur ek ek karke matke me daalne laga. Dheere dheere paani upar aane laga. Aakhir paani itna upar aa gaya ki kauwa aaram se pee saka. Kauwe ne paani piya aur khushi se ud gaya. Seekh: mehnat aur samajh se har mushkil hal ho jaati hai.

Sher aur Chuha

Ek jungle me ek bada sher rehta tha. Ek din woh ped ke neeche so raha tha. Tabhi ek chhota chuha uske upar idhar udhar daudne laga. Sher ki neend khul gayi aur usne chuhe ko apne panje me pakad liya. Chuha darr gaya aur bola, maharaj mujhe chhod do, ek din main aapki madad karunga. Sher hans pada. Itna chhota chuha meri kya madad karega? Phir bhi usne chuhe ko chhod diya. Kuch din baad sher ek shikari ke jaal me phans gaya. Woh zor zor se dahadne laga. Chuhe ne awaaz suni aur turant wahan pahuncha. Usne apne tez daanton se jaal kaat diya. Sher azaad ho gaya. Sher ne chuhe ko dhanyawad diya. Seekh: kisi ko chhota mat samjho, har koi kabhi na kabhi kaam aata hai.

Khargosh aur Kachhua

Ek khargosh ko apni tez daud par bahut ghamand tha. Woh roz kachhue ka mazaak udata tha. Ek din kachhue ne kaha, chalo daud lagate hain. Khargosh hans pada aur maan gaya. Daud shuru hui. Khargosh bahut aage nikal gaya. Usne peeche dekha, kachhua bahut door tha. Khargosh ne socha, thoda aaram kar leta hoon. Woh ek ped ke neeche so gaya. Kachhua dheere dheere chalta raha, bina ruke, bina thake. Jab khargosh ki neend khuli, kachhua finish line ke paas tha. Khargosh tezi se bhaaga, par der ho chuki thi. Kachhua jeet gaya. Seekh: dheere aur lagataar chalne wala hi jeet ta hai.

Lalchi Kutta

Ek kutte ko kahin se roti ka tukda mila. Woh use muh me dabaye ghar ki taraf chal pada. Raste me ek nadi thi aur nadi par ek pul tha. Pul par chalte hue kutte ne paani me apni parchhai dekhi. Use laga paani me doosra kutta hai jiske muh me bhi roti hai. Lalach me aakar usne socha, ye roti bhi main le leta hoon. Jaise hi usne bhaunkne ke liye muh khola, uski apni roti paani me gir gayi. Kutta khaali haath reh gaya. Seekh: lalach buri bala hai.

Chatur Lomdi aur Angoor

Ek din ek bhookhi lomdi jungle me ghoom rahi thi. Use ek bel par lage mithe angoor dikhe. Angoor bahut upar the. Lomdi ne uchhal kar angoor todne ki koshish ki, par nahi pahunch payi. Usne baar baar koshish ki, par har baar haar gayi. Aakhir thak kar woh boli, ye angoor to khatte hain, mujhe chahiye hi nahi. Aur woh chali gayi. Seekh: jo cheez na mile use bura kehna aasaan hota hai.

Ekta me Bal

Ek kisaan ke chaar bete the. Chaaron aapas me hamesha ladte rehte the. Kisaan bahut pareshan tha. Ek din usne lakdiyon ka ek gattha mangaya aur beton se kaha, ise todo. Har bete ne koshish ki, par koi gattha nahi tod saka. Phir kisaan ne gattha khol kar har bete ko ek ek lakdi di. Sabne aasaani se lakdi tod di. Kisaan bola, dekho, jab tum saath ho to koi tumhe tod nahi sakta. Akele rahoge to koi bhi tod dega. Beton ko baat samajh aa gayi. Us din ke baad woh kabhi nahi lade. Seekh: ekta me hi bal hai.

Sona Dene Wali Murgi

Ek gaon me ek kisaan aur uski patni rehte the. Unke paas ek jaadui murgi thi jo roz ek sone ka anda deti thi. Dheere dheere woh ameer ho gaye. Par kisaan ko lalach aa gaya. Usne socha, murgi ke pet me bahut saare ande honge, ek saath nikal leta hoon. Usne murgi ko maar diya. Par pet me koi anda nahi tha. Ab roz ka ek anda bhi band ho gaya. Kisaan bahut pachtaya. Seekh: zyada lalach me jo hai woh bhi chala jaata hai.

Chalak Bandar aur Magarmachh

Nadi ke kinare jamun ka ek ped tha. Us par ek bandar rehta tha. Nadi me ek magarmachh rehta tha. Bandar roz magarmachh ko meethe jamun deta tha aur dono dost ban gaye. Magarmachh ki patni ne socha, jo bandar itne meethe jamun khata hai, uska dil kitna meetha hoga. Usne magarmachh se bandar ka dil laane ko kaha. Magarmachh bandar ko apni peeth par bitha kar nadi me le gaya aur beech me sach bata diya. Bandar ne dimaag lagaya aur bola, arre mera dil to ped par rakha hai, wapas chalo, le aata hoon. Magarmachh use wapas le gaya. Bandar ped par chadh gaya aur bola, moorkh, dil bhi kahin ped par rakha jaata hai? Aaj se hamari dosti khatam. Seekh: musibat me bhi dimaag thanda rakho.
